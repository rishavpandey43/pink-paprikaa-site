# Voice & Accessibility

## Voice

A confident city café that speaks Hinglish without apology. The brand's one supplied line sets the
tone — _India's First Desi Urban Café_: a claim, stated flatly, no hedging.

Talk to the guest as **you**; the café speaks as **we**. Never "the customer", never "users".

### The hard rules

- **`Pink Paprikaa`** — two `a`s. A misspelling is a content bug, not a typo.
- **Casing.** Title Case for names, claims and buttons of consequence. Sentence case for body,
  helper text and form labels. ALL CAPS only for overlines and heat labels — never a full sentence.
- **Numbers.** `₹` with no space, no decimals on whole rupees: `₹240`. Ranges use an en dash:
  `₹180–₹320`. Times are 12-hour lowercase: `8am – 11:30pm`.
- **Interface labels are plain English.** Spice is Mild / Medium / Hot / Extra Hot; sizes are
  Regular / Sharing. Hinglish belongs to dish names and voice, never to a control a guest must
  decode.
- **No emoji. Ever** — including 🌶 for spice. That is what `SpiceLevel` is for.
- At most one exclamation mark per screen, and never in a heading.
- **Never say:** artisanal, curated, experience (as a noun), elevated, journey, unleash,
  revolutionise. Never call the food "authentic" — the claim is _desi_, which is a fact, not a
  compliment.
- Being pure veg is stated once, plainly, and never over-sold: "100% vegetarian kitchen."

## Accessibility

Every component ships an axe assertion in its test and runs the same engine again in Storybook,
where real CSS is available and colour contrast can actually be measured. The a11y addon is set to
`test: "error"` — a violation fails the story, it does not sit in a panel.

### What the system guarantees

- **Focus is a brand decision.** The base layer paints `:focus-visible` as a 2px pink outline at 2px
  offset, everywhere. No component re-states it, and none can quietly drop it.
- **Hit targets are 44px minimum** (`--layout-hit-min`), including icon-only buttons whose glyph is
  20px.
- **Disabled is a real grey fill**, never a reduced opacity — opacity fails contrast and reads as
  "loading" rather than "unavailable".
- **Behaviour comes from Radix.** Dialogs, tabs, accordions, tooltips, selects and switches inherit
  correct roles, keyboard handling and focus management rather than hand-rolled approximations.
- **Reduced motion is global.** `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` collapses animation and transition
  durations in the base layer.

### What each component owns

- A **name**: an icon-only control takes a label; a decorative icon is `aria-hidden` so its meaning
  is not announced twice beside the text it decorates.
- **Never colour alone.** A status colour always arrives with a message. `SpiceLevel` and `DietMark`
  both carry text alternatives — heat and diet are legally and practically load-bearing information.
- **Heading order.** `Text` picks a sensible element per step, but the page owns its outline: pass
  `as` when the visual level and the document level differ.
- **Contrast.** `text-text-muted` and lighter are for secondary content on light grounds only. On a
  flooded pink or ink panel, use `text-on-brand` / `text-on-inverse`.
