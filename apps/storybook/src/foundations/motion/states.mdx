# Interaction states

Anything a guest can press has five states. Hover darkens one step (pointer only, never on touch).
Press darkens a second step and scales to 0.97 in 80ms; Space/Enter show it too. Focus is
keyboard-only: a 2px ring, white on coloured surfaces. Disabled is a real grey, never just opacity.
All components read one hook, `usePress`, and the `--state-*` tokens.
